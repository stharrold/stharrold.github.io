[Clip 12] Before I looked at a single spending number, I wrote down every question I'd ask, every test I'd run, and the exact words I'd use for each possible result. Then I posted it publicly. It's called pre-registration. Think of calling your shot in pool: eight ball, corner pocket. Why bother? Because these files are public, and if you slice data enough ways, something will look exciting just by chance. Researchers fall into that trap without meaning to. Calling the shot means you can tell a planned test from a lucky find.
