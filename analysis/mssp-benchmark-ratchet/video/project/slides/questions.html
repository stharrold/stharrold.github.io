The plan

Five questions, fixed in advance

Here are the questions I committed to. Question one was the main question, chosen in advance as the one that counts most: were the early renewers in places where care costs more than their target? That's the regional pull, the room for the neighborhood boost. A yes would be evidence for suspect A, the better-target story, in a form that isn't just past savings in disguise. Questions two and three are context. Was each group's own spending above or below its target? And were early renewers already saving more? Question four puts the factors together: which ones made a group more likely to renew early? Question five asks whether renewing changed anything: did spending go up or down afterward? For each one, the plan spelled out in advance what each result would mean. So I'm not choosing my words after seeing the numbers.
