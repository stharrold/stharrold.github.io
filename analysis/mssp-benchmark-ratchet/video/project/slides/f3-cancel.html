[Clip 18a] Here's what happens when you put them together. The blue bar is the regional boost. Early renewers stood to gain a little over one and a half points more than stayers. [Clip 18b] The orange bar is the own-spending catch. They stood to lose about two points more, because they'd been saving more. [Clip 18c] Add them up, and you get the grey bar: about minus half a point, with a range that crosses zero. The boost and the penalty cancel. It's our eleven thousand and twenty-five example, in real data. [Clip 18d] After the study, I checked what actually happened, using Medicare's published 2022 targets. Early renewers' new targets came in about one point three percent lower on average, and two thirds of them went down. That check wasn't in the original plan, so I'm labeling it as an add-on. But it agrees. So on average, a reset didn't hand these groups a better target.
