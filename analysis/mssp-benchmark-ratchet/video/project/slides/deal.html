[Clip 05] Let's use one made-up example with round numbers. Medicare sets this group's target at eleven thousand dollars per patient per year. The group's patients actually cost ten thousand five hundred. That's five hundred dollars saved per patient, or seven and a half million dollars across fifteen thousand patients. How much does the group keep? On the common track, up to half: about three point seven five million. On the top track, called ENHANCED, seventy-five percent: about five point six million. Same savings, almost two million dollars more. Remember that gap.
