[Clip 20a] Finding five: did renewing change spending? Compared with groups that hadn't renewed yet, early renewers' spending growth was about the same: minus zero point four percent, with a range from minus four to plus two and a half. [Clip 20b] So we can rule out a drop bigger than about four percent, or a rise bigger than about two and a half. But I can't call it "no effect." I had only one earlier period to compare trends, during COVID, and a trend that started before renewal could hide a small effect. My plan said not to call it an effect in that case, so I don't.
