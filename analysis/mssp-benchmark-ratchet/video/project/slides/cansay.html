What this study can and cannot say

Can say

  • Which groups chose to reset early
  • What a reset stood to do to their targets, on average
  • Spending didn't shift by more than a few percent

Cannot say

  • That renewing caused anything
  • Which reason was "the" reason
  • What early renewal cost Medicare
[Clip 21] So what can this study say? It shows which groups chose to reset, what a reset stood to do to their targets, and that spending didn't shift by more than a few percent. What it can't say: that renewing caused anything, since groups chose for themselves; which suspect was the real reason; or what any of this cost Medicare.
