[Clip 01] In January 2022, twenty-nine groups of doctors and hospitals did something odd. They quit their Medicare contracts early and signed new ones, which reset the targets Medicare uses to decide whether they earn a share of savings. By Medicare's own numbers, checked after the study, two thirds of them ended up with a lower target. A more difficult one. So why do it?
